# IDENTITY-001 recommendation

## Recommended next milestone

Proceed to **ACTUALITY-001 — Relativistic actuality and state individuation**.

IDENTITY-001 has exhausted what follows from `medium = universe`: one physical referent, no automatic reification of its representations, and—when combined with CONTINUUM-STATE-001—one complete realized state per admissible state position. The remaining branch cannot be closed by restating identity.

## Exact mission

Formulate and audit the missing **relativistic actuality principle**:

> Specify whether actuality attaches to one complete state, a growing ordered segment, or a complete history; define physical identity across gauge-, coordinate-, and admissibly refoliation-related representations; and preserve V11's operational Lorentz invariance and one-metric effective description.

## Required gates

1. Define a complete, gauge-invariant continuum state including matter, constraints, correlations, branch/memory data, and clock/ruler content.
2. Define when two slice descriptions represent the same physical state or the same physical history.
3. Select single-state, growing-history, or whole-history actuality by an explicit principle rather than by vocabulary, a diagram, or a coordinate label.
4. If one current state is privileged, supply an invariant selector or prove that any fundamental selection is operationally hidden in the V11 regime.
5. If a growing or whole history is selected, distinguish its asserted ontic status from the mathematical existence of a solved trajectory.
6. Keep actuality logically independent from determinism, probabilities, reversibility, entropy, causal accessibility, and reconstruction unless separately derived.
7. Connect to TIME-001 only through its still-missing relational clock-to-proper-time identification.
8. Introduce no constitutive law, field equation, parameter, observational fit, or modification of V11.

## Development decision

PBUF may state:

> The spacetime medium is the physical universe itself. Consequently, there is one physical bearer of the complete continuum state, and the mathematical space of admissible states is not thereby a plurality of realized universes.

PBUF may also state, using the inherited continuum result:

> At each admissible state position, the one continuum realizes one complete state and not two incompatible complete configurations.

PBUF should not yet state that only one state across history is physically actual, that a unique global present exists, or that the full history is merely representational. Those claims await ACTUALITY-001.
