# IDENTITY-001 explicit consequence register

## Derived from identity alone

| ID | Consequence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| I-1 | “Spacetime medium” and “physical universe” refer to one physical entity. | Direct identity consequence |
| I-2 | A physically instantiated property of the medium is a property of the universe; no separate universe-state sits behind the medium-state. | Direct substitution of identicals |
| I-3 | Coordinate systems, state spaces, reference states, ensembles, histories, and solution sets do not become additional universes merely because they represent the real medium. | Identity/representation distinction |
| I-4 | An ontological rule cannot consistently assign one actuality status to the medium and a conflicting status to the universe. | Consistency consequence |
