# PBUF MEDIUM-001 — Constitutive response of the spacetime medium

## Decision

**Outcome D: the exact missing constitutive principle is isolated.** The current V11 framework does not uniquely derive a constitutive law. It fixes a homogeneous elastic contribution `Omega_sigma(a)` and retains standard GR, but supplies neither a covariant microscopic deformation variable nor a local/nonlocal medium action. Consequently it does not determine elastic moduli, a response kernel, a matter-loading projection, or a medium-to-effective-geometry map.

Conditional on choosing a medium field and action, covariance, minimal matter coupling, and conservation restrict the response to the variational family below. This is a restricted admissible family, not a selection of one member. No new free parameter, V11-alpha redefinition, observational fit, photon law, or weak-lensing modification is made.

## 1. Derived, assumed, and missing statements

**Derived from accepted inputs:** matter loads the relativistic interface through the full `T_m^{mu nu}`; for minimal coupling its generalized load is the functional chain-rule projection in MED-001-E02. Linear response is the Hessian of the medium action and, where invertible, deformation is obtained using its causal Green function. Effective total stress-energy must satisfy the covariant Noether identity.

**Structural assumptions used only to describe the admissible family:** existence of a differentiable medium variable `chi^A`, an action/effective action, a background about which response can be linearized, and a later effective metric map. Locality, isotropy, scalarity, two-derivative truncation, and instantaneous response are *not* derived and are invoked only for the MB-001 limiting example.

**Missing from V11:** the ontology and tensor type of `chi^A`; its normalization and dimensions; `S_med`; `g_eff[chi]`; the coarse-graining map; kernel state/boundary conditions; and the matching statement that reproduces both V11's homogeneous `Omega_sigma(a)` and Einstein dynamics. This combined specification is the missing principle.

## 2. Admissible constitutive variables

A deformation must be an observable change relative to a specified medium state, not merely the already-effective metric. Covariance permits several inequivalent choices: scalar order parameters; material-coordinate/displacement fields whose covariant gradients define strain; symmetric tensor strain/order fields; or a collection `chi^A` containing internal Quantum Engine degrees of freedom. Gauge-redundant variables require constraints and quotienting before the response operator can be inverted.

V11 selects none of these. A scalar is inadequate for generic anisotropic stress and gravitational tensor modes unless it is explicitly only a projection. A displacement field introduces a reference/material structure not supplied by V11. A symmetric tensor can carry shear but is not thereby derived. Thus the fundamental deformation variable is **undetermined**, and the neutral notation `chi^A` is a placeholder index, not a new physical postulate.

## 3. Loading by stress-energy

Let standard matter couple minimally to the later physical metric: `S_m[g_eff[chi],Psi]`. With

`T_m^{mu nu}=(2/sqrt(-g)) delta S_m/delta g_mu_nu`,

functional differentiation gives MED-001-E02. This fixes normalization once `g_eff[chi]` is known and forbids inserting a separate matter-medium coupling. A local algebraic metric map makes the kernel local; a derivative or nonlocal map makes it derivative/nonlocal.

The full tensor is therefore the fundamental loading operator. Trace loading, rest-frame energy density, pressure, momentum flux, or anisotropic stress are possible projections generated by different maps; none is selected by V11. Dust can hide this distinction, while radiation and anisotropic sources expose it. `rho` alone is not a covariant fundamental load.

## 4. Most general constitutive response presently derivable

For a covariant medium action `S_med[chi;Q_V11]`, where `Q_V11` denotes only already-fixed microphysical inputs, equilibrium/dynamics has the variational form MED-001-E01. About a solution, its exact first-order constitutive operator is the Hessian kernel MED-001-E03. The response form MED-001-E04 follows if a gauge-fixed retarded inverse exists.

This formulation includes local elasticity, derivative elasticity, memory, dispersion, and spatial nonlocality. A local derivative expansion corresponds schematically to a delta-supported kernel containing algebraic, two-derivative, and higher-derivative covariant operators. The coefficients must be derivatives of the missing V11 medium functional; naming them would not derive them. Stability requires an appropriate positive static quadratic form, hyperbolic/causal dynamics requires retarded support and a well-posed principal part, and reciprocity follows for a nondissipative Hessian. Dissipation requires an open-system/influence-functional completion and cannot be inferred from the background density.

MB-001-E05 is recovered only after assuming a dimensionless scalar projection, staticity, isotropy, locality through two spatial derivatives, and positive constants. Its Helmholtz Green function is spatially nonlocal even though the differential operator is local. V11 derives neither `K`, `G`, `s`, nor `ell`; in particular `ell=sqrt(G/K)` cannot be set from source morphology.

## 5. Local versus nonlocal response

Locality belongs to the operator, not necessarily its solution. A local action yields a differential `K_AB(x,y)` supported at coincidence, while inversion propagates a load nonlocally through `G_R`. Genuine constitutive nonlocality arises from integrated-out microscopic correlations or memory and appears directly in off-diagonal kernel support. Covariance and causality constrain either case but do not choose between them. V11's homogeneous `Omega_sigma(a)` samples only zero-wave-number background behaviour and cannot reconstruct frequency or wave-number dependence.

## 6. Effective GR limit

GR is a later matching description, as required by the mission. A candidate medium theory passes the GR limit only if coarse graining supplies `g_eff[chi]`, its effective action is diffeomorphism invariant, and variation gives MED-001-E06 with the standard Newton normalization already retained by V11. The homogeneous solution must reproduce V11's `Omega_sigma(a)`, perturbations must yield the Einstein constraint and propagation structure over the claimed macroscopic regime, extra modes must decouple or be consistently hidden, and the total Ward identity must hold.

V11 assumes the effective Einstein description; it does not derive these matching conditions from a medium action. Agreement of one Friedmann background is necessary but not sufficient: infinitely many actions and kernels share the same homogeneous stress history while differing in shear, waves, stability, and local response.

## 8. Conservation-law audit

Matter conservation follows on shell from the standard diffeomorphism-invariant matter action. A covariant medium action has its own Noether identity; at the effective interface the sum of matter and medium stress-energy is conserved. Separate conservation is not automatic if the sectors exchange energy-momentum. The functional source and medium equation must be varied from one common action for the exchange terms to cancel identically.

The MB-001 static balance has an integral compatibility relation but is not a time-dependent continuity equation. Calling its source a conserved mass charge or its flux an energy-momentum flux would add unsupported physics. Full entries and limitations are in `conservation_law_audit.csv`.

## 9. Identifiability proof

Knowledge of `Omega_sigma(a)` restricts an effective stress only along the homogeneous cosmological history. Terms that vanish on that history—shear invariants, Weyl-sensitive terms, transverse gradients, higher derivatives, and kernels with the same zero-mode value—can be added without changing it. Hence the map from constitutive functionals to the V11 background is many-to-one. Covariance, dimensions, and conservation impose identities but do not eliminate this null space. A unique constitutive law is therefore not derivable from the accepted inputs.
