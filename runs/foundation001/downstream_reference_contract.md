# FOUNDATION-001 downstream reference contract

## Canonical authority

All post-FOUNDATION-001 development shall cite the frozen ontology from `runs/foundation001/foundational_ontology.md` using `PBUF FOUNDATION-001, FP-n`.

| Postulate | Accepted downstream input | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| FP-1 | The universe is one continuous, non-embedded physical spacetime medium. | Do not demote the medium to a merely instrumental representation or add an external substrate. |
| FP-2 | Gravity is an emergent medium response. | A future derivation must still specify the mathematical response; the postulate is not itself a field equation. |
| FP-3 | Time is an emergent ordering parameter for successive medium states, not a fundamental medium dimension. | A future derivation must recover operational duration and satisfy FP-5; do not treat the postulate as the completed clock construction. |
| FP-4 | One complete physical configuration is occupied at each admissible evolutionary state; listed mathematical structures are representational unless derived otherwise. | Do not reopen competing actuality ontologies or reify a state space, history, reference state, or solution set by notation alone. |
| FP-5 | The recovered effective theory must satisfy V11 operational relativistic limits. | Every proposed dynamics, clock, metric, or observable must pass this compatibility gate. |
| FP-6 | No new free fundamental constant or auxiliary coupling is allowed without derivation or observational necessity. | Identify every introduced coefficient's status and justification; a change of notation does not constitute a derivation. |

## Downstream claim labels

To keep axioms separate from results, subsequent documents should classify foundational dependencies as:

- **Postulate:** accepted directly from a numbered FOUNDATION-001 statement.
- **Derived consequence:** established from identified postulates plus stated mathematical or physical inputs.
- **Additional authorized assumption:** not contained in FP-1 through FP-6 and permitted only by the milestone authority.
- **Compatibility condition:** a gate, especially FP-5 or FP-6, which constrains but does not by itself derive a model.

## Revision gate

Revision of FP-1 through FP-6 is out of scope unless a milestone explicitly requests ontology review, a contradiction is demonstrated, or a failed prediction requires revision. Any such revision must name the affected postulate and preserve the distinction between changing an axiom and correcting a downstream derivation.
