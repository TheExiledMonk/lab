# PBUF FOUNDATION-001 — Freezing the foundational ontology

## Status

**Decision: frozen for subsequent development.** The statements below are explicit framework postulates. They are accepted design inputs, not claimed mathematical theorems. Their validity is to be evaluated through internal consistency, derived consequences, and predictive success.

Beginning with the milestone following FOUNDATION-001, development shall derive mathematical, physical, and observational consequences from these postulates. The postulates shall not be re-proved, replaced, broadened, or reopened unless:

1. a later milestone explicitly authorizes an ontology review;
2. a contradiction is demonstrated; or
3. a failed prediction requires revision.

## Foundational postulates

### FP-1 — One physical spacetime medium

The physical universe consists of one continuous spacetime medium. The medium is not embedded in another physical substrate and is not merely a mathematical representation.

### FP-2 — Emergent gravity

Gravity is an emergent response of the spacetime medium rather than a fundamental interaction.

### FP-3 — Emergent time

Time is not a fundamental physical dimension of the medium. Time is an emergent ordering parameter describing the evolution of successive states of the medium.

### FP-4 — One complete configuration per state

At each admissible state of evolution, the spacetime medium occupies one complete physical configuration. Mathematical state spaces, histories, reference configurations, and solution sets are representational tools unless explicitly derived otherwise.

### FP-5 — V11 relativistic compatibility

The effective relativistic description recovered by PBUF must remain compatible with the V11 operational limits, including local Lorentz invariance and observed relativistic phenomena.

### FP-6 — No unauthorized fundamental constants

No additional free fundamental constants or auxiliary couplings may be introduced unless independently derived from the ontology or required by observation.

## Development rule

For all subsequent milestones, FP-1 through FP-6 are accepted inputs. Coding and research agents shall not attempt to re-prove, replace, or broaden them unless the milestone explicitly requests an ontology review or one of the revision conditions in the Status section is met.

Derived claims must remain distinguishable from these postulates. A downstream result may cite a postulate as a premise, but shall not relabel that postulate as a theorem or claim that FOUNDATION-001 derived it from earlier mechanics or mathematics.

## Permitted future work

Subject to FP-1 through FP-6, future milestones may derive:

- deformation variables;
- stored-energy functionals;
- constitutive laws;
- field equations;
- cosmological consequences;
- quantum consequences; and
- observational predictions.

## Forbidden development actions

Subsequent work shall not:

- reopen ontology-selection debates;
- replace these postulates with alternative metaphysical frameworks;
- introduce new ontological assumptions without explicit authorization;
- reinterpret these postulates as derived theorems; or
- evade FP-6 by renaming an independently adjustable constant or coupling.

## Authority and naming

FOUNDATION-001 is the canonical frozen-ontology artifact for development after this milestone. It is distinct from the historically referenced but unavailable `FND-001` artifact noted in ALPHA-ARCH-001. FOUNDATION-001 does not reconstruct, supersede, or supply missing historical alpha-traceability evidence for that artifact.

V11 remains authoritative within its established scope. FP-5 makes compatibility with its operational relativistic limits an explicit gate on future derivations; FOUNDATION-001 itself changes no V11 equation or prediction.

## Success criterion and completion

The milestone is complete because subsequent development can cite FP-1 through FP-6 directly as fixed premises. The stable citation form is:

`PBUF FOUNDATION-001, FP-n`

where `n` is the postulate number. Downstream documents shall identify any additional premise separately and shall not attribute it to FOUNDATION-001 without explicit authorization.
