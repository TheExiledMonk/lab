# TIME-001 recommendation

## Recommended next milestone

Proceed to **TIME-002 — Relational clock and covariance closure**, as a principle-selection audit rather than a dynamics derivation.

TIME-002 should not write a new gravitational or medium field equation. It should require an authoritative choice or derivation of:

1. the complete instantaneous state and its gauge quotient on the three-dimensional carrier;
2. the non-temporal admissibility/orientation relation defining physical histories;
3. reparametrization equivalence, so the history label is not an absolute time;
4. an internal clock subsystem or functional and its domain of monotonicity;
5. the interface to GEOMETRY-002's single normalized Lorentzian metric;
6. a foliation-independence test showing that distinct admissible slicings yield the same effective observables;
7. a universal-clock test showing that matter clocks, photon propagation, and metric proper time agree in the V11 regime; and
8. an information audit classifying microscopic evolution as invertible, coarse-grained, dissipative, stochastic, or causally incomplete before making any arrow-of-time or past-reconstruction claim.

## Acceptance gates

TIME-002 succeeds only if it can state, without a new free parameter:

- what constitutes a complete physical state;
- which structure orders its possible successors;
- which quantities are invariant under relabeling of that order;
- how a clock reading is constructed from state correlations;
- why that reading matches the proper time of the one effective metric; and
- why the construction preserves V11's operational Lorentz invariance and GR limit.

If the metric map remains open, TIME-002 should remain blocked at the clock-to-proper-time gate and feed its requirements into GEOMETRY-002. Cosmological `a`, `H(a)`, V11 thermal variables, or CORE-001's conditional relaxation parameter must not be repurposed as fundamental time.
