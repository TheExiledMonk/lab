# PBUF GEOMETRY-001 — Emergence of the effective metric

## Decision

**Outcome D, with the mathematical non-uniqueness of Outcome C.** The current authoritative PBUF corpus does not define a microscopic medium variable `chi`, and it contains no constitutive map from such a variable to the physical metric. Consequently no unique `g_{mu nu}[chi]` can be derived. Several inequivalent maps satisfy all presently stated requirements.

The exact missing principle is a **normalized, diffeomorphism-equivariant constitutive law that identifies the microscopic medium's clock and ruler content with one Lorentzian metric**. It must specify the field content and dimensions of `chi`, the unloaded state, the map `G: chi -> g`, its derivative order or kernel, and any gauge redundancy. Its normalization must be fixed by the V11 Quantum Engine or another authoritative premise, not by a new response coefficient.

This result leaves V11, standard GR, the standard matter action, the restored alpha hierarchy, and the frozen weak-lensing implementation unchanged.

## 1. Authoritative premises and boundaries

V11 section 1.1 says that special relativity, GR, Lorentz invariance, Einstein's field equations, and standard quantum dynamics remain intact. Its elastic picture is explicitly a constitutive interpretation. Sections 2.2--2.3.3 provide homogeneous, scale-factor-dependent background quantities `alpha_QM`, `alpha_resolved`, `alpha_T(a)`, `epsilon_0,T(a)`, and `Omega_sigma(a)`. They do not introduce a local microscopic field, spacetime indices for a medium variable, a local elastic action, or a metric map.

`T_m^{mu nu} = (2/sqrt(-g)) delta S_m/delta g_{mu nu}`

and shows that its medium projection is generated by the metric map. ARCH-001 removes `g_dev` as a PBUF coupling. MB-001's scalar balance remains conditional. PHOTON-001's optical metric is an equivalent representation of a chosen refractive law, not a derivation of the physical metric.

The V11 alpha quantities therefore enter only their existing background and thermal roles. None is licensed as a coefficient in `g[chi]`.

## 2. What can `chi` be?

No unique answer follows from V11. At minimum, a candidate `chi` must contain enough covariant information to construct a nondegenerate rank-two Lorentzian tensor. A bare scalar cannot do this without a prior metric, preferred frame, or additional tensorial structure. The admissible ontology classes are:

1. `chi` already contains a symmetric nondegenerate tensor (metric/strain class); the problem becomes its normalization and physical identification.
2. `chi` contains four coframe fields `E^a_mu`, permitting `g_mu nu = eta_ab E^a_mu E^b_nu`; local Lorentz redundancy must then be specified.
3. `chi` contains material scalars or embedding fields whose covariant derivatives form an induced metric; rank, signature, and the temporal degree of freedom require additional postulates.
4. `chi` contains a scalar plus a normalized medium four-velocity and a spatial metric/projector, allowing distinct temporal and spatial responses.
5. `chi` is nonlocal or collective, so the metric depends on a coarse-grained functional rather than on a point field.

CORE/FND's historical three-component lattice variable cannot by itself define a four-dimensional Lorentzian metric, and its direct matter vertex was retired by ARCH-001. It is not an authoritative choice for `chi`.

## 3. Most general V11-compatible ansatz

Let `chi^A` denote an unspecified collection of covariant microscopic fields, with `A` including all internal and tensor labels. The most general map is a functional

`g_mu nu(x) = G_mu nu[chi](x)`,

subject to

`G[f^* chi] = f^* G[chi]`, `det G != 0`, and `signature(G)=(-,+,+,+)`.

The first condition is diffeomorphism equivariance; it is the precise covariance condition on an emergence map. A general quasilocal derivative expansion is

`G_mu nu[chi] = G_mu nu^(0)(chi) + G_mu nu^(1)(chi,nabla chi) + G_mu nu^(2)(chi,nabla chi,nabla nabla chi,...)`.

This notation is a classification, not a proposed new theory. The coefficient functions and even the covariant derivative require constitutive input. If a connection is not independently included in `chi`, writing `nabla` using the output metric makes the definition implicit and requires a well-posedness condition. The fully nonlocal class is

`g_mu nu(x) = integral K_mu nu A(x,y;chi) chi^A(y) d mu_chi(y) + nonlinear terms`,

with a causal/covariant kernel. V11 supplies neither such a kernel nor its measure.

Representative local zeroth-derivative realizations expose the degeneracy:

- Tensor realization: `g_mu nu = H_mu nu(chi_tensor)`, with arbitrary invertible signature-preserving tensor function `H`.
- Coframe realization: `g_mu nu = eta_ab E^a_mu E^b_nu`.
- Isotropic medium realization: `g_mu nu = A(I) h_mu nu - B(I) U_mu U_nu`, where `U` is the medium velocity, `h` its spatial metric, and `I` denotes scalar medium invariants.
- Conformal realization, only after a reference metric is supplied: `g_mu nu = A(chi) gbar_mu nu`.
- Disformal scalar realization, only after a reference metric is supplied: `g_mu nu = A(chi,X) gbar_mu nu + B(chi,X) partial_mu chi partial_nu chi`.

The functions `A`, `B`, `H`, and `K` are not allowed additions to PBUF here; they display the missing functional freedom. Choosing them would be a new constitutive postulate. A scalar conformal map is not “most general,” while an isotropic medium with a rest frame already permits independent clock and ruler responses.

## 4. Symmetry, locality, isotropy, and dimensions

### Covariance

The map must commute with diffeomorphisms. Matter must couple minimally and universally to the single output metric in `S_m[g,Psi]`; otherwise V11's standard-GR premise and the equivalence principle would need an explicit amendment. If `chi` has internal gauge redundancy, `G` must be invariant under it. Covariance restricts tensor form but does not select `chi` or the constitutive functions.

### Lorentz invariance and isotropy

The unloaded solution must admit a locally Minkowski metric. Exact local Lorentz invariance forbids an observable preferred direction, but a medium rest frame can be present only if its observable effects are compatible with V11's stated operational Lorentz invariance. Spatial isotropy in that frame reduces a rank-two map to temporal and spatial projectors; it does not force their two responses to coincide. Parity removes pseudoscalar terms but does not close the remaining functions.

### Locality and derivative order

V11 states no locality or derivative-order axiom for emergence. An algebraic map yields a pointwise matter source. A derivative map yields derivatives of `T^{mu nu}` after integration by parts. A nonlocal map yields a source kernel. These lead to inequivalent continuum laws, so locality cannot be silently assumed.

### Dimensional consistency

With length-valued coordinates, `g_mu nu` is dimensionless. Hence every term in `G_mu nu[chi]` must be dimensionless. V11 does not state `[chi]`, a microscopic length for derivative terms, or a normalization converting strain/occupancy to metric response. The background quantities are dimensionless density fractions or thermal pipeline inputs and do not supply this missing normalization. A dimensionally explicit map therefore cannot currently be written without an unsupported scale or coefficient.

## 5. Required weak-field limit

For an unloaded homogeneous configuration `chi_0`, the map must satisfy `G[chi_0] = eta_mu nu` locally (or the standard cosmological GR background on large scales). For `chi=chi_0+delta chi`,

`g_mu nu = gbar_mu nu + h_mu nu`,

`h_mu nu(x) = integral R_mu nu A(x,y) delta chi^A(y) d mu(y) + O(delta chi^2)`,

where `R = delta G/delta chi |chi_0`. This response kernel—not a new constant—is the missing linearized content of the constitutive law.

Because Einstein's equations remain intact, the resulting metric must obey the standard linearized Einstein equation (with the V11 elastic sector included in the total stress-energy), the standard Newtonian limit `g_00=-(1+2 Phi/c^2)+O(Phi^2/c^4)`, and the corresponding GR spatial curvature needed for standard light propagation. The Bianchi identity must be compatible with total stress-energy conservation. These are necessary tests of a proposed map, not enough to reconstruct `R`: field redefinitions, conformal/disformal responses, derivative responses, and modes invisible in a chosen limit remain degenerate.

## 6. Exact downstream chain

The admissible action-level architecture is

`S = S_EH[g] + S_sigma[g,chi; V11 microphysics] + S_m[g,Psi]`.

For the standard minimally coupled matter action and a general functional map,

`delta S_m/delta chi^A(x) = (1/2) integral d^4y sqrt(-g(y)) T_m^{mu nu}(y) delta g_mu nu(y)/delta chi^A(x)`.

Defining the matter source in the `chi` equation by

`J_A(x) = -(1/sqrt(-g(x))) delta S_m/delta chi^A(x)`,

gives

`J_A(x) = -(1/(2 sqrt(-g(x)))) integral d^4y sqrt(-g(y)) T_m^{mu nu}(y) delta G_mu nu(y)/delta chi^A(x)`.

For a local algebraic map this reduces to

`J_A = -(1/2) T_m^{mu nu} partial G_mu nu/partial chi^A`.

Thus `T^{mu nu}` is fixed, while the trace, energy-density, pressure, or tensor projection is fixed only by `delta G/delta chi`. No independent matter coupling constant is permitted or needed.

If `C` is the normalized covariant-to-static coarse graining and `P` the projection of the `chi` equation into the MB scalar sector, then

`u = C[chi,g]`,

`s(x) = P_x^A[J_A]`,

and only after the unknown elastic action is reduced may one obtain

`K u - div(G grad u) = s`.

Here `G` in the final equation is MB-001's gradient stiffness, not the metric functional `G_mu nu`. Neither `s`, `K`, nor the gradient stiffness is presently derived. Writing `s(rho)` prematurely discards pressure, momentum flux, and anisotropic stress.

## 7. What becomes derivable once the map is known?

Immediately from a fully normalized `g[chi]` one obtains the matter source functional `J_A`, the exact stress-energy projection that loads each medium mode, its local/derivative/nonlocal character, and the universal photon and matter trajectories from the same metric. One also obtains the linear response kernel and can test the Newtonian, post-Newtonian, causal, equivalence-principle, and Lorentz limits.

The map alone does **not** derive the medium dynamics. `S_sigma` (or equivalent microscopic dynamics) is still required for a field equation, propagator, stiffness, stability, and dispersion. A normalized coarse-graining/projection is still required for `u` and `s`. Once those are supplied together, `K`, the MB gradient stiffness, the propagation length, boundary conditions, continuum source, and photon equation follow rather than being independently assumed.

## 8. Uniqueness assessment

Current symmetries exclude malformed maps but do not choose among tensor, coframe, induced, conformal, disformal, derivative, and nonlocal realizations. Even within the static isotropic local class, independent temporal and spatial responses remain. The standard weak-field limit fixes combinations of their first derivatives around one state, not the nonlinear map.

Therefore a unique implementation is not authorized. The precise missing derivation is not an algebraic step hidden in V11; it is the absent constitutive identification of microscopic clocks and rulers with `g_mu nu`, together with the local elastic dynamics and normalized reduction needed to reach MB-001.
