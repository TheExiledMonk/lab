# PBUF MATTER-MEDIUM-INTERACTION-001 — Physical Origin of Elastic Loading

## 0. Decision

**Outcome C: the frozen one-medium ontology does not uniquely determine the
matter–medium interaction.** It does, however, sharply determine what any such
interaction can mean.

Matter cannot be a substance outside or alongside the spacetime medium. At one
order state, every physical distinction attributed to matter must already be a
gauge-invariant distinction in the complete medium configuration \(q\). In a
regional or sectoral accounting, elastic loading can therefore only be an
**internal transfer of generalized placement work/momentum between a
matter-bearing aspect of that configuration and the elastic placement
accounting of the same medium**. Its resultant is the single placement-load
covector already identified by NATIVE-SOURCE-001:

\[
 \mathcal S_{\rm ext}\in (T_y\mathcal Y_0)^*,\qquad
 \delta W_{\rm load}[\eta]=\langle\mathcal S_{\rm ext},\eta\rangle .
 \tag{MMI-001}
\]

“External” here means external to the selected elastic subsystem or region,
not external to the universe. In a complete closed one-medium accounting,
internal exchange contributions must cancel. Thus no force carrier, second
medium, or gravitational substance is required or permitted.

This work-transfer statement is a necessary interpretation of a native load;
it is **not yet a physical law that constructs the load**. The frozen
milestones do not decide which invariant feature of \(q\), or which
stress-energy data used to describe that feature, performs placement work.
They also do not decide whether the work form is exact, local, additive, or
species-independent. A normalized universal virtual-work/interaction
postulate remains necessary. Its placement variation would generate
\(\Pi_{\rm source}\), but the postulate is not contained in the frozen corpus.

No conclusion below uses MATTER-001 as a premise, introduces microscopic
ontology, derives a metric map, changes V11, or fits observations.

## 1. Matter interpretation audit

The decisive frozen statements are FP-1 and STATE-003: there is one physical
medium, and \(q\) is its entire instantaneous physical state. Therefore

\[
 \boxed{\text{matter is an already-contained physical organization or
 distinction of }q,\text{ not an additional occupant of the medium}.}
 \tag{MMI-002}
\]

This is the strongest unique interpretation available. The component
realization of \(q\) is unknown, so MMI-002 cannot be strengthened into a
specific microscopic or field identification.

| Candidate interpretation | Classification | Frozen reason |
|---|---|---|
| localized deformation | admissible, not necessary | deformation \(C[q,q_0]\) is a relational observable and may describe a matter-bearing configuration, but the frozen corpus never identifies all matter with \(C\); doing so would also confuse a possible source description with the deformation solved from balance |
| localized energy concentration | admissible only conditionally, not necessary | stored elastic energy \(W(C)\) is defined, but a universal total matter energy density is not; BALANCE-001 requires kinetic, clock, action, and source closure before such an energy is available |

| separate material substance embedded in the medium | contradiction | violates FP-1 and STATE-003 by adding an ontic sector outside the complete one-medium configuration |

Accordingly “stable excitation” is useful language only when explicitly
conditionalized. The foundational result is not that matter has been derived
as a soliton, wave, defect, strain packet, or energy lump. It is that whatever
matter is, it is a physical pattern/aspect of the one complete medium state.

This also answers the motivating question. No particular scalar property—rest
mass, stored energy, total energy, or amplitude—is proven to be what necessarily
perturbs the medium. What is necessary is relational: a matter-bearing part or
description perturbs the elastic accounting only insofar as it performs
nonzero generalized work on admissible placement variations.

## 3. Elimination report

### 3.1 Necessary core

Assume matter loads the accepted placement balance. By NATIVE-SOURCE-001 and
SOURCE-PROJECTION-001, the effect must pair with every admissible placement
variation as a covector. It cannot enter first as \(W\), \(C\), or \(P_F\).
Therefore its invariant mechanical meaning is work/momentum transfer into the
placement sector. By FP-1, the transfer cannot come from an external physical
substrate; by BALANCE-001 it is an internal exchange under complete accounting.
This proves the necessary core stated in MMI-001.

The proof is conditional on there being nonzero elastic loading. It does not
prove that every configuration distinction called matter must load the elastic
sector. That universal nonzero-coupling claim is precisely among the missing
physics.

### 3.2 Merely admissible mechanisms

Energy exchange, wave scattering, stress divergence, boundary traction, and
an exact interaction action can each realize generalized work transfer once
their extra prerequisites are supplied. None follows because:

- stored elastic energy is not a universal total energy or exchange current;
- wave support and propagation are conditional on unfrozen kinetic/evolution
  closure;
- stress-energy does not map to a spatial placement covector without a
  pullback, direction/derivative, partition, and normalization;
- boundary data represent an exterior but do not define a bulk coupling; and
- a general load one-form need not be exact, so balance alone does not imply an
  interaction potential or action.

These mechanisms are alternative realizations or consequences of a coupling,
not frozen explanations of it.

### 3.3 Contradictions and exclusions

The following are eliminated:

- matter as an ontically separate substance, carrier, or medium;
- a separate gravitational force channel;
- direct insertion of matter into \(W\), \(C\), or elastic stress while calling
  it the already-frozen source;
- a universal algebraic scalar-to-vector body-force rule on the isotropic
  unloaded branch, because no direction or inverse length is available;
- identification of regional boundary loading with the universal physical
  origin; and
- claims that deformation, energy, or wave character is uniquely matter's
  loading property.

The first three contradict frozen structure. The latter three are eliminated
as purported necessities, although suitably completed versions may remain
admissible models.

## 4. One-medium consistency proof

Let the complete state be (q\in\mathcal Q_{\rm phys}), and let “matter” and
“elastic placement” denote two accounts or projections of physical distinctions
already contained in \(q\). Suppose their interaction contributions to a
selected balance are (\mathcal S_{\rm m\to e}) and
(\mathcal S_{\rm e\to m}). BALANCE-001 requires internal exchanges over a
complete closed partition to cancel:

\[
 \mathcal S_{\rm m\to e}+\mathcal S_{\rm e\to m}=0
 \quad\text{in the appropriate total weak accounting}. \tag{MMI-003}
\]

The elastic account retains the first term as its load. Equation MMI-003 does
not create two media; it is bookkeeping within one complete configuration.
Only one placement response is solved, all regular bulk contributions combine
in one \(b\), and boundary terms describe flux across a selected regional
boundary. No independent gravitational substance or propagation field is
present. Gravity remains the response of the medium under FP-2.

STATE-003 is preserved because every instantaneous operand and observable of a
future interaction must be a gauge-basic functional of \(q\) (or relationally
of \(q,q_0\)); no state datum is appended to \(q\). A future formula may expose
components of an authorized realization, but it may not treat an interaction
variable as additional instantaneous ontology.

## 5. Universality assessment

Three levels of universality must not be conflated:

| Level | Status |
|---|---|
| ontological universality: all matter/radiation are configurations of the same medium | necessary from FP-1 and STATE-003 |
| channel universality: every elastic effect lands in the same placement dual and resultant balance load | necessary from the frozen native balance |
| law universality: one normalized functional maps every admissible excitation to loading by the same rule | not derived |

Ordinary matter, electromagnetic radiation, and other admissible excitations
cannot obtain separate media or displacement fields. Nevertheless, the frozen
premises allow a joint nonlinear rule, partition-specific rules with one
resultant, or a universal rule of total stress-energy. They do not establish
equal coupling, additivity, or even nonzero coupling of every admissible
excitation.

One-medium ontology therefore favors a universal interaction principle as the
minimal closure and makes it sufficient to avoid species-by-species new laws,
but it does not logically entail that principle. Universality must be stated as
part of the additional postulate. In particular, universality cannot be
inferred merely by naming total stress-energy as the input.

## 6. Weak-field compatibility

The surviving work-transfer principle is structurally compatible with the
retained V11 weak-field behavior because a small interaction load can enter the
already-frozen linearized elastic balance without changing the constitutive
law:

\[
 \mathcal L_0 u=\delta\mathcal S_{\rm int}. \tag{MMI-004}
\]

A gauge-basic, locally Lorentz-compatible, universal interaction functional
could supply (\delta\mathcal S_{\rm int}), while the still-unselected metric
map could complete the retained V11 composite response. Nothing in internal
work exchange conflicts with a one-metric relativistic limit, and radiation
can use the same resultant channel without a new carrier.

This is permission, not recovery. V11 constrains the composite of source
response, elastic response, and metric map. Since the last remains unselected,
V11 cannot determine the interaction rule or its normalization separately.
No metric, trajectory, or empirical coefficient is derived here.

## 7. Mathematical consequences of the physical principle

If the additional physical principle is formulated as a gauge-basic
interaction functional using only authorized variables,

\[
 \mathcal A_{\rm int}[y,\text{matter represented within }q],
 \qquad
 \langle\Pi_{\rm source},\eta\rangle
 =-D_y\mathcal A_{\rm int}[y,\text{matter}]\eta,
 \tag{MMI-005}
\]

then its natural mathematical consequences are:

1. **dual-space output:** the first placement variation is automatically an
   element of \((T_y\mathcal Y_0)^*\), with regular bulk and boundary
   representatives when integration by parts permits them;
2. **exact work form:** the load is variationally exact, with mixed-variation
   reciprocity/integrability wherever the functional is twice differentiable;
3. **exchange identity:** invariance of the complete coupled functional yields
   the relevant Noether exchange identity, with internal transfers cancelling
   in total;
4. **naturality:** gauge, frame, relabeling, and electromagnetic-gauge
   invariance constrain the derivative to an equivariant natural operator;
5. **operator order:** a nonzero isotropic matter-only bulk load cannot be a
   point-local algebraic map from a scalar or symmetric rank-two tensor to a
   covector; it must obtain direction from an authorized derivative,
   nonuniform configuration, or boundary/functional dependence;
6. **support class:** locality of the interaction density would produce a
   finite-jet differential operator (and boundary terms), while a genuinely
   functional interaction would produce a nonlocal generalized covector;
7. **linearization:** the weak-field source is the mixed second variation of
   the interaction functional about the reference branch; and
8. **single resultant:** contributions from all admissible matter descriptions
   must combine in the same placement covector, whether or not the functional
   is additive.

MMI-005 is the preferred *class* because it directly expresses reciprocal
work transfer. It is not forced by the frozen balance: dissipative or otherwise
non-exact generalized work forms remain admissible unless reversibility or a
full coupled action is separately postulated. Consequently this milestone does
not select differential order, contractions, support, sign, normalization,
linearity, or the final operator.

## 8. Readiness for SOURCE-PROJECTION-002

The milestone is not ready for a unique derivation of
\(\Pi_{\rm source}\) from the frozen premises alone. It has reduced the missing
physics to one precise additional postulate:

> **Universal matter–placement work postulate.** Every admissible
> matter-bearing configuration of the one medium couples to the elastic
> placement sector through one normalized, gauge-basic work rule, stated using
> only authorized functionals of \(q\) (and an authorized comparison state when
> relational), with its locality/support, pullback, reversibility/exactness,
> and treatment of all excitation classes explicitly fixed.

If an exact reversible interaction is intended, the postulate must provide
\(\mathcal A_{\rm int}\) of MMI-005. If exactness is not intended, it must
instead provide the generalized virtual-work one-form directly and state the
exchange/dissipation accounting. Merely asserting that “matter is energy,”
“matter is deformation,” or “all energy gravitates” is insufficient: none
specifies the required covector, direction, index bridge, support, or
normalization.

Once this postulate is supplied, SOURCE-PROJECTION-002 may derive the operator
as its placement variation and test uniqueness. Until then multiple physically
distinct interaction laws remain possible, even though all share one ontology
and one resultant load channel.

## 9. Completion theorem

**Theorem (MATTER-MEDIUM-INTERACTION-001).** Under exactly the listed frozen
milestones, matter is necessarily an organization/distinction of the complete
one-medium state \(q\), not a second substance. If it elastically loads the
medium, the effect is necessarily represented as internal generalized
placement-work/momentum transfer into the single native load channel. The
premises do not determine whether matter is universally deformation, energy,
wave content, or another specific excitation class; do not determine which
matter invariant performs the work; and do not determine exactness, locality,
normalization, or species universality. Hence the physical interpretation of
loading is fixed, but the physical interaction law is not. A universal
normalized matter–placement work postulate remains necessary before
\(\Pi_{\rm source}\) can be uniquely determined. \(\square\)

This completes the requested audits without altering V11 or introducing new
ontology, fields, constants, constitutive laws, metric maps, or observational
inputs.
