# KINEMATICS-001 reference-state definition

The unloaded reference is the gauge-equivalence class

`R_0 = {q_0 modulo diffeomorphisms and internal gauge | C[q_0,q_0]=1}`.

It is defined by zero **relative** deformation, not by zero components of the primitive field. Every representative must be nondegenerate, orientation/signature admissible, locally compatible with the V11-retained Minkowski geometry in a freely falling frame, and homogeneous/isotropic when used as a cosmological background.

Three inequivalent choices remain:

1. a fixed material reference pulled through cosmological evolution;
2. the instantaneous FLRW natural configuration; or
3. a temperature-dependent family `R_0(T,a)` inherited from V11 microphysics.

V11 does not choose among them. The choice determines whether background expansion counts as deformation and cannot be treated as a notation convention. V11's `alpha`, `epsilon_0`, `Omega_sigma`, saturation function, and `Rmax` retain their published meanings; none is redefined as a reference strain or limiting stretch.
